# Experience Compiler (EC) Convergence Handoff

Status: v0.2.1 reconciliation input

## Canonical Role

EC is the persistent knowledge, memory, learning and advisory substrate for enterprise consulting intelligence. LLMs operating with EC are replaceable reasoning engines that use this substrate to provide enterprise advisory and consulting capabilities.

EC + its LLM(s) are **advisors/consultants**, not enterprise executives, management authority, operational truth, or an automatic decision/execution authority.

Depending on enterprise need, the advisory capability may specialize as management consulting, strategy consulting, business/process consulting, financial consulting, supply-chain consulting, manufacturing/operations consulting, quality consulting, IT consulting, data consulting, security consulting, implementation consulting, operations/maintenance consulting, and other specialist consulting roles.

### Lifecycle role

During initial enterprise-system establishment, EC + its LLM(s) primarily act as **implementation consultants**: understand the enterprise, consume EVO/Eidos public contracts, compile/propose enterprise packages and experience intent, assist validation/configuration/migration/testing/training/go-live, and diagnose implementation gaps.

After go-live, EC + its LLM(s) primarily act as **ongoing advisory and operations/maintenance consultants**: observe governed enterprise information, diagnose, recommend, research, maintain knowledge, propose governed changes, support continuous improvement, and assist business/IT operations and maintenance.

### Runtime independence

EC intelligence is an enhancement layer, not a prerequisite for normal enterprise-system execution.

**EVO + Eidos must remain a fully operable enterprise system when EC and all LLMs are absent or unavailable.** Already-published enterprise definitions, authorized Commands, deterministic business execution, governed facts, and deterministic human interaction must continue normally without EC inference.

Loss of EC/LLM availability may remove or degrade advisory, diagnosis, recommendation, research, learning and improvement capabilities, but must not corrupt or invalidate the already-published deterministic EVO + Eidos runtime.

EVO's and Eidos's project/development LLMs are engineering actors for developing and maintaining those products; they are not runtime participants in a customer's enterprise business processes.

## EC Owns

- Knowledge/provenance and evidence
- Enterprise/industry memory
- Cases, decisions, outcomes, lessons, patterns
- Learning and learning methods
- Research
- Context compilation
- Reasoning, diagnosis, recommendation
- Management Intelligence
- SOP / Metric / Flow / Capability Intelligence
- Scenario/hypothesis design and simulation interpretation
- Enterprise Intelligence Packs
- Enterprise Function Packs
- Knowledge Merge
- Experience Proposal production
- Command Proposal and Operational Change Proposal production

These ownership statements describe EC's advisory/intelligence artifacts and capabilities. They do not grant EC authority over EVO operational truth, enterprise approval, or business execution.

## Required EVO Boundaries

- Enterprise Context for governed current/versioned operational understanding.
- Enterprise Observation for facts/outcomes/changes used by the learning loop.
- Command Proposal admission into the normal EVO Command path.
- Operational Change Proposal governance for learned improvements.
- Enterprise Simulation for deterministic consequence calculation.
- Enterprise Package Schema/Capability Discovery so EC can compile to an EVO-owned package contract rather than invent a parallel format.
- Enterprise Package Validate/Deploy/Definition-Only Export boundaries for governed implementation and portability workflows.

EC may propose or compile inputs for these boundaries. EVO remains responsible for validation, authorization, publication, operational truth and deterministic execution.

## Required Eidos Boundary

EC produces high-level, versioned Experience Proposals. Eidos validates and deterministically realizes them. EC does not generate arbitrary executable Eidos runtime code as the normal boundary.

Eidos remains usable without EC for already-defined/published deterministic enterprise experiences.

## Advisory Authority Invariant

Advisory capability does not imply decision or execution authority.

`Observe/Research -> Analyze/Diagnose -> Recommend/Propose -> Human/Policy Decision -> Validation/Authorization -> EVO Execution`

EC + its LLM(s) may observe, analyze, diagnose, research, forecast, simulate, recommend and propose. Enterprise decisions, approvals, delegations and operational authority remain with authorized humans/policies and EVO's governed execution boundary.

## Learning Invariant

EC learning output is not operational truth.

`Learning -> Recommendation/Proposal -> Validation -> Authorization/Governance -> Published Version/Command -> EVO execution`

EC must preserve provenance, scope, time/version and uncertainty/contradiction state when learning from operational observations.
